# Suno — soundtrack license basis (batch 01)

Applies to every file under `assets/audio/music/game/` and the corresponding
masters in `audio_masters/suno_batch_01/`.

- **Source:** Suno (https://suno.com/), Custom Mode, Instrumental on. Prompt and
  Exclude text for each track are recorded in
  `docs/audio_soundscape_and_suno_brief.md`.
- **Licence basis:** Suno's Terms of Service grant the generating account
  ownership of / a commercial-use licence to output created while on a **paid
  plan**. See https://suno.com/terms and https://help.suno.com (Ownership &
  Commercial Use). Output generated on the free plan is non-commercial and does
  **not** become commercial retroactively when the account upgrades.
- **Required credit:** none contractually required. The manifest therefore lists
  `required_credit = None` for these rows. PAIN TAXI still lists the soundtrack
  in the in-game credits screen as "Original soundtrack generated with Suno".
- **Compliance action before ship:** confirm the generating account was on an
  active paid plan on the render date (2026-07-16) and archive the plan receipt
  plus the Suno song links / track IDs alongside
  `audio_masters/suno_batch_01/music_manifest.csv`. The song links were not
  supplied with the batch handoff and are the one outstanding provenance gap.

The full Suno Terms of Service are not redistributable verbatim here; consult
the URLs above for the authoritative text.
