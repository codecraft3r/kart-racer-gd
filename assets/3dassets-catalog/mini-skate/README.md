# Mini Skate

Miniature 3D skate park kit with low-poly, flat-shaded ramps, halfpipes, rails, benches, grind boxes, and skateboards in a coral-orange and periwinkle-blue palette. Two chunky cartoon skater characters pose mid-trick against a dark checkered background, giving the pack a playful, toy-like, isometric feel.

## Stats
- Asset count: 20
- Archive: `kenney_mini-skate.zip` (120 files)
- Formats: GLB, FBX, OBJ (+ MTL, colormap PNG textures 512x512)
- Includes: PBR color textures, preview renders, overview HTML

## Visual style
Low-poly, flat-shaded 3D models with chunky proportions and a stylized cartoon read. Warm coral-orange ramps contrast with cool periwinkle-blue and soft-purple surfaces, accented by light wood planks and charcoal/off-white details. Composition reads as a small, posed toy diorama.

## Best for
- Skateboarding or extreme-sports arcade games
- Casual mobile / 2.5D platformers
- Level prototyping for park-style stages
- Toy diorama scenes, character showcases, marketing renders
