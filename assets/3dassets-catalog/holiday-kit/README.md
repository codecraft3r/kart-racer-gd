# Holiday Kit

A festive low-poly Christmas village kit with cabins, snow-capped pine trees, snowmen, a decorated tree with presents, reindeer, sleds, lampposts, and snow drifts. Built in Kenney's signature flat-shaded "mini" isometric style with bright, saturated colors and clean colored polygons — no textures, just stylized geometry perfect for cozy holiday scenes.

## Stats

- **Assets:** 100 (per source; 99 unique 3D models in archive)
- **3D formats:** GLB, FBX, OBJ + MTL
- **Preview renders:** PNG (RGB and transparent, 918×515 and 1920×1080)
- **Archive contents:** 518 files across `Models/`, `Previews/`, and license/doc files

## Visual style

Low-poly, flat-shaded, isometric, cartoon. Toy-like miniature diorama with Christmas-tree green, pure white snow, warm brown wood, red gift/bunting accents, golden star and lantern highlights, cool grey rocks, and pale-blue snow shadows. No baked textures — all color comes from per-face vertex colors or solid materials. Optimized for stylized holiday and winter scenes.
