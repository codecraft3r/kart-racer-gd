# Furniture Kit

Low-poly isometric furniture pack (140 assets) — a fully furnished home with bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, living room, dining area and office, all in Kenney's signature clean miniature style. Ships as 3D models plus 702 pre-rendered PNG sprites in four isometric directions.

## Stats

- **Assets:** 140 models
- **Total files:** 1557
- **By extension:** png 702, glb 140, fbx 140, obj 140, dae 140, stl 140, mtl 140, url 3, txt 1
- **Top-level folders:** Isometric (561), Models (849), Side (141)
- **Sprite format:** indexed PNGs ~37×68 to 76×96 px, four isometric angles + side views

## Visual style

Isometric (2:1) low-poly 3D with flat shading and soft warm-wood tones. Palette centers on peach/light-orange wood (`#F2C28A`), white walls and ceramics, red upholstery (`#D9534F`), and a dark slate floor (`#3A4047`) with small green plant accents. Geometry is simplified and silhouette-driven, ideal for top-down or 2.5D scenes. Renders include both full 3D sources and pre-baked PNGs so the pack drops into 2D engines without a 3D pipeline.

## Best for

- Isometric room and home builders
- Top-down life sims and casual games
- City builder and shop/office interiors
- Architectural mockups and floorplan visualization
- Educational or onboarding scenes that need cozy, recognizable furniture
