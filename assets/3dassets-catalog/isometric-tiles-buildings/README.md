# Isometric Tiles Buildings · Kenney

A pack of clean, blocky low-poly isometric buildings — apartment blocks, shops, a hospital, fire station, street and sidewalk tiles — perfect for casual city builders and tycoon games.

- Asset count: 128

- Format breakdown: 132 PNG, 1 TXT (license), 1 XML, 2 URL (social)
- Structure: `PNG/` (tile assets), `Spritesheet/`, plus `Preview.png`, `Sample.png`, `License.txt`, `Donate.url`, `Facebook.url`
- Sample tile dimensions: 132×127 (buildings), 99×54 (props), 918×515 (preview)

## Visual style
Low-poly isometric tiles with flat shading and clean cartoon edges. Palette is dominated by a bright blue backdrop with cream/tan building walls, red and brick-brown accents, dark gray asphalt, and small green tree props. Buildings have simple window grids and subtle roof detailing. Perspective is a fixed 2:1 isometric, ideal for tile-based grid placement.
