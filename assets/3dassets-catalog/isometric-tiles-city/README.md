# Isometric Tiles City

A clean, low-poly isometric tile set from Kenney for assembling modular cities. Cubic blocks in a 2:1 dimetric projection snap together to form roads with lane markings and crosswalks, low-rise buildings with flat or stepped roofs, parks with blocky trees, water tiles, sidewalks, and small street details. The soft, approachable palette and crisp white linework make it well suited to casual city builders, tycoon prototypes, and isometric strategy games.

## Stats

- Asset count: 128
- ZIP files: 150 total entries (142 PNG, 2 XML, 2 URL, 1 TXT)
- Top-level layout: `PNG/` (129 tiles), `Details/` (12 detail sprites), `Spritesheet/` (5 sheets), `Preview.png`, `License.txt`
- Sample tile format: 32x32 RGBA, 2:1 dimetric projection

## Visual style

- Projection: true isometric / 2:1 dimetric
- Geometry: low-poly, voxel-like cubic blocks with stepped silhouettes
- Rendering: flat-shaded faces with subtle hand-drawn white line accents
- Palette: sky blue background, sand and tan walls, dark asphalt, leafy green, water blue, white linework
- Mood: friendly, modern-arcade, casual

## Best for

- City-builder and tycoon game prototypes
- Isometric strategy or tactics games
- Simulation and management demos
- Board-game digital adaptations
- Level mockups and greyboxing for 2.5D scenes
