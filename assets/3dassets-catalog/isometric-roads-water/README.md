# Isometric Roads Water

The "Water Works" expansion to Kenney's isometric road tile pack — 40 dimetric (2:1) tile sprites for laying roads across rivers, canals, and waterfalls, designed to dock seamlessly with the base isometric roads pack on a shared diamond grid.

## Stats

- Asset count (per og_description): 40
- Zip files: 48 total (42 PNG, 1 SVG, 1 SWF, 1 AI, 1 JPG, 1 readme.txt, 1 macOS resource fork)
- PNG sprite dimensions: 100x80 (4), 100x77 (4), 100x65 (24), 100x58 (10) — all on a 2:1 isometric grid
- Tile families: bridges (low/high, banked/flat, EW/NS), river segments (banked, slope, waterfall, hill — N/E/S/W), slope tiles (N/E/S/W), waterfall end pieces

## Visual style

Clean flat-shaded isometric sprites on a soft beige background. Roads are dark asphalt gray with thin white lane markings; water is a calm sky-blue with a slightly deeper blue band; grass tops are a fresh mid-green; banked/dirt edges are warm sandy brown. Subtle gradients on water and grass add a touch of depth without breaking the flat, modular look. Tiles snap together on a 2:1 dimetric grid.

## Best for

- City-builder and tycoon games (roads, rivers, canals)
- Isometric strategy and simulation games
- Modular tile-based maps needing bridges, river crossings, slopes, and waterfalls
- Game-jam prototypes that need a complete water/road kit fast
- Pairing with the base Kenney isometric roads pack for full coverage
