## How to import a pack

1. Open the pack's `README.md` / `preview.png` to confirm it fits.
2. Download the zip from the `download_url` (or the bundled Starter-Kit-Racing for track pieces).
3. Extract into `assets/kenny_<slug>/` (matches the existing `assets/kenny_car-kit` convention) and re-open in Godot to generate `.import` files.
