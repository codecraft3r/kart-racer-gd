# Retro Urban Kit

Isometric 3D low-poly urban environment kit featuring weathered multi-story brick and wood buildings, an industrial scaffolding tower, delivery truck, dumpsters, autumn-leaved trees, cracked asphalt streets, sidewalks, fencing, and overhead power lines. Hand-painted diffuse textures give a gritty, lived-in, retro/post-apocalyptic mood across a muted earthy palette of brick red, faded green, weathered brown and concrete gray accented by orange autumn foliage.

## Visual style

- Isometric camera, low-poly geometry
- Hand-painted diffuse textures with weathering, dirt and rust detail
- Gritty, abandoned / lived-in urban mood
- Muted, earthy palette: brick red, weathered brown, faded green, concrete gray, asphalt gray, rust, off-white, autumn orange foliage
- Modular pieces designed to tile and stack into larger city blocks
