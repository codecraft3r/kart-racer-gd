# Animated Characters Protagonists

A pack of four stylized 3D cartoon protagonists — a criminal male, cyborg female, skater female and skater male — sharing a single rigged medium-character mesh with painted skin textures and three looping animations, ready to drop into any 3D engine.

## Stats

- **Asset count:** 8
- **Category:** 3D models
- **Sub-category:** Animated characters
- **Files in archive:** 20 (4 FBX, 5 PNG, 4 SVG, 1 TXT, 2 URL, 4 directories)
- **Skin textures:** 4 × 1024×1024 RGBA PNG with matching SVG source
- **Base mesh:** Model/characterMedium.fbx
- **Animations:** Animations/{idle,jump,run}.fbx
- **Format:** FBX (mesh + animation), PNG (skin textures), SVG (texture source)

## Visual style

Low-poly stylized 3D characters with a flat, painterly texture look — rounded forms, big heads, simple silhouettes. The pack reads as a unified cast rather than four separate heroes, since they share a single base rig and only differ by skin.

- Style tags: low-poly, cartoon, modern, stylized, flat
- Background mood: cool dark navy
- Character palette anchors: muted red (#E93031), denim blue (#165077), warm skin tone (#F49573), accent green on the alien logo, plus clean white and deep black
