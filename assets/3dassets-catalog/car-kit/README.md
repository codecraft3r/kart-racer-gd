# Car Kit

A collection of 45 low-poly 3D vehicles and modular car parts in OBJ, FBX, and GLB formats, designed for games that need charming, performant transport. The pack includes cars, trucks, ambulances, race cars, tractors, a green garbage truck, a small droid, plus detachable parts such as wheels, bumpers, cargo boxes, and traffic cones.

## Stats

- 45 assets
- 268 files in archive
- 54 PNGs (3 colormaps at 512x512, preview at 918x515, plus 64x64 thumbs)
- Formats: OBJ (+MTL), FBX, GLB
- Includes HTML overview and license

## Visual style

Low-poly isometric vehicles with flat-shaded faceted geometry. The palette is bright and saturated — oranges, greens, blues, yellows, and reds with white and gray accents for cabs and tires. Reads cleanly at small sizes and works well with mobile and stylized PC art.

## Best for

- Racing and driving games
- City builders and traffic sims
- Vehicle customization and garage mechanics
- Casual mobile games and prototypes
