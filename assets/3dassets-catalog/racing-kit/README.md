# Racing Kit

Stylized isometric 3D racing-track kit: looping asphalt circuits, curbs, grandstands, pit buildings, bridges, ramps and small toy cars — everything you need to block out a complete racecourse diorama in minutes.

## Stats
- 110 assets (per source description)
- 1,274 files in the archive
- 587 PNG textures (327 distinct dimensions, mostly small 200–310 px icon-sized images)
- 112 models shipped in five formats: DAE, FBX, GLB, OBJ (+ MTL) and STL
- CC0 licensed (free for personal, educational and commercial use)

## Visual style
- Isometric 3D, low-poly, miniature "tabletop" feel
- Clean toy-like geometry with simple materials and soft shading
- Color palette: dark slate background, light grey asphalt, saturated red/white curbs and grandstands, leafy greens, plus yellow/blue/red car accents
- Cozy, friendly arcade mood — works equally well for serious racing prototypes and casual mini-games

## Best for
- Top-down or isometric racing games
- Track-builder prototypes and level design mockups
- City/town dioramas that need a racetrack landmark
- Arcade-style driving mini-games
- Game-jam starter kits and learning projects for 3D pipelines (glTF, FBX, OBJ)
