# Watercraft Kit

A low-poly 3D asset pack of stylized boats, ships, and harbor props by Kenney, including two ocean liners, a submarine, galleons, tugboats, speedboats, sailboats, fishing boats, rowboats, buoys, docks, gates, and shipping containers. Ships in OBJ+MTL, FBX, and GLB with a shared PBR colormap, ideal for casual and mid-core maritime games.

## Stats

- **Assets:** 45 (per official page; zip contains 46 model files when both an `arrow` and `arrow-standing` marker are counted)
- **Zip total entries:** 248
- **Formats:** OBJ + MTL (46), FBX (46), GLB (46)
- **Textures:** 50 PNGs (shared 512x512 PBR colormap + per-model preview thumbnails 64x64)
- **Docs:** `License.txt`, `Overview.html`, URL shortcuts to docs/Patreon
- **Preview image:** 918x515 PNG

## Visual style

Isometric, low-poly 3D models on a dark checkered backdrop. Geometry is chunky and faceted, with matte PBR materials and a single shared colormap texture for the whole kit. Bright, saturated palette (red, teal-green, blue, yellow, orange, white, brown-wood, gray) with crisp white sails and red-orange ship striping. No rigging, no animations — static, game-ready, model-per-asset approach.
