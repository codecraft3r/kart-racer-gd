# Racing Pack

A top-down 2D cartoon asset pack for building arcade-style racing games. Includes cars in multiple colors, paved track pieces with red-and-white curbs, grandstands with spectators, a pit lane, tents, traffic cones, trees, rocks, and road barriers — everything needed to assemble a complete top-down racing level.

## Stats

- Asset count: 420
- ZIP entries: 456
- PNG sprites: 429
- Sprite sheets: 9
- Vector sources (SVG): 3
- Sample sprite dimensions: cars ~70×130 px, small props ~40×70 px, scene previews ~918×515 px

## Visual style

Top-down perspective, flat-shaded cartoon art with clean vector-like shapes and bold outlines. Vibrant saturated palette over a bright grass-green ground plane: red/white striped curbs, multi-colored cars (red, yellow, blue, green, black), and warm accent props (orange cones, brown dirt). Style is consistent with the rest of Kenney's 2D top-down packs — friendly, readable, and well-suited to mobile and casual arcade rendering.
