# Shooting Gallery

A vibrant 2D cartoon shooting-gallery asset pack by Kenney. The preview shows a fairground/carnival stage framed by red curtains with a "TIME UP!" banner, green bushes, a blue water/pond foreground, and wooden stall elements. Targets include classic red/white ring targets, a multi-color bullseye target, and cute yellow rubber-duck targets, with HUD overlays for score (+10 popup, rubber-duck score icon), a crosshair, and an ammo counter. Flat color fills with thick black outlines give a playful, kid-friendly mood suited to casual target-shoot mini-games.

## Visual style

Cartoon, flat-shaded 2D sprites with thick black outlines and a bright, saturated carnival palette. Iconography is friendly and rounded (chibi ducks, chunky ring targets, bubble-letter banners), avoiding realism in favor of immediate readability. Consistent 4-direction/top-down-friendly framing across targets and stall pieces.

- **Palette:** orange, green, blue, red, yellow, white, brown
- **Style tags:** cartoon, flat, 2d, vibrant, thick-outline, fairground, carnival, kids-friendly, casual
- **Sample dims:** 918×515 preview; HUD icons around 42–54 px
