# Isometric Roads

A modular isometric tile set by Kenney covering roads, water, beach, hills, bridges, parking lots, and trees. Tiles are designed to snap together at cardinal and corner directions to build continuous road networks and natural terrain for casual city/town sims.

## Stats

- Asset count: 95
- Zip contents: 102 files
  - 96 PNG tiles (RGBA, ~100x58-65 px rhombus footprints)
  - 1 source SVG, 1 Adobe Illustrator (.ai), 1 SWF
  - 1 preview.jpg, 1 readme.txt
- Naming convention: directional suffixes encode orientation (N, S, E, W, NE, NW, SE, SW)
- License: CC0 (public domain)

## Visual style

- 3/4 axonometric (true isometric) projection on a rhombus footprint
- Clean vector-flat look with no outlines, 2-tone cell shading
- Low-poly, chunky bevelled edges with a thin soil/dirt sidewall visible on terrain tiles
- Bright, saturated but not neon palette anchored on grass green, asphalt grey, water blue, and warm sand tan
- White painted stripes and curb details on road and lot tiles for instant readability

## Best for

- Isometric city and town builders
- Casual mobile strategy or tycoon games
- Transport / road puzzle games
- Tile-based world maps where the same tile snaps in 8 directions
- Low-poly prototype mockups and greybox-to-art upgrades
