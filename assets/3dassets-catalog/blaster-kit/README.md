# Blaster Kit

A collection of 40 low-poly 3D weapon models — chunky cartoon blasters, pistols, rifles, rocket launchers, scopes, targets and projectiles — supplied in OBJ, FBX and GLB along with a shared PBR texture set.

## Stats

- 40 3D weapon models
- 220 total package files
- 46 PNGs (512x512 textures + 918x515 previews)
- 40 FBX, 40 OBJ, 40 GLB models
- 1 colormap.png, 1 variation-a.png texture
- License: CC0 1.0

## Visual style

Low-poly, chunky cartoon weapons with crisp angular silhouettes. Bright, candy-bright palette of purple, lime green, golden yellow, orange, lavender, gunmetal grey and accent red on a deep navy backdrop. Clean diffuse-only PBR textures at 512x512, no normal/roughness/metalness maps — fully self-contained, no external texture dependencies. Soft hand-painted highlights, faceted shading and isometric-friendly presentation.

## Best for

Sci-fi shooter prototypes, top-down twin-stick arenas, casual mobile FPS, stylized roguelikes, educational / classroom game jams, placeholder hero weapons, and any project that wants a complete, attribution-free blaster roster drop-in.
