# Survival Kit

Low-poly 3D model pack of 80 camping and wilderness survival props, ready to drop into stylized outdoor scenes.

## Stats

- 80 3D models (tents, watchtower, campfire, log pile, fence, pine trees, rocks, lantern, axe, signpost, bed, bench, tarp, bottles, skulls and more)
- Formats included: FBX, GLB, OBJ (+ MTL)
- Texture: 512x512 colormap (P-mode palette) plus a color variation
- Total files in archive: 421 (87 PNGs, 80 FBX, 80 GLB, 80 MTL, 80 OBJ, plus License/Preview/Docs)

## Visual style

Low-poly, flat-shaded diorama look with bold, saturated cartoon colors. Smooth rounded silhouettes and chunky proportions read clearly at gameplay scale. The scene combines forest greens and warm autumn oranges with weathered brown wood, cool grey-blue rocks and sandy beige ground - balanced and friendly rather than gritty. The Kenney signature "blocky but cute" aesthetic with subtle hand-painted texture variation. Suits mobile, casual and stylized PC/console games.

## Best for

- Stylized survival, camping or base-building game prototypes
- Open-world forests, campsites, and outpost scenes
- Mobile hyper-casual and low-poly PC/console titles needing drop-in props
- Game-jam, MVP, and educational/interactive 3D scenes
- VR/AR dioramas or Blender/Three.js demos
