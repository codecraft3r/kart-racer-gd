# Coaster Kit

A modular 3D roller-coaster and monorail track kit by Kenney. 180 low-poly, game-ready track pieces (curves, straights, banks, loops and connectors) ship with FBX, OBJ, GLB and PNG textures across six coaster styles: Monorail, Steel, Wood, Flume, Hanging and Mouse. Designed to snap together for theme-park or racing-style games in Unity, Unreal, Godot or any engine that reads FBX/OBJ/GLB.

## Stats

- **Assets:** 180
- **Archive files:** 939 (183× FBX, 183× OBJ, 183× GLB, 183× MTL, 193× PNG, plus docs/previews)
- **Texture:** 512×512 indexed colormap
- **Previews:** 918×515 PNG per coaster style
- **Source:** https://kenney.nl/assets/coaster-kit

## Visual style

Low-poly, flat-shaded 3D models in Kenney's signature clean, colorful aesthetic. Tracks render as pale lavender / off-white rails on a dark navy-blue checkerboard staging background. The accompanying colormap introduces warm accents — oranges, pinks, whites and greys — for per-piece tinting. Geometry is chunky and forgiving, with smooth bevels and visible joint markers so pieces read clearly even at small scales.

## Best for

- Racing or track-based mini-games
