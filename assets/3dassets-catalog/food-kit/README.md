# Food Kit

A cheerful 3D low-poly pack of 200 food, drink, cookware, and kitchen-utensil models delivered in FBX, GLB, and OBJ formats. Items span raw ingredients (fruits, vegetables, meat, fish, dairy), prepared dishes (pizza, burgers, sushi, cake, soup, hot dogs, donuts), kitchenware (pots, pans, plates, cutting boards, bottles, cans), and hand tools (forks, knives, spoons, cleaver) — ideal for cooking sims, restaurant games, and food-themed prototypes.

## Visual style

Low-poly 3D models rendered in a bright, friendly isometric look. Surfaces use flat or lightly-shaded solid colors with clean geometric silhouettes — no baked PBR detail, just chunky shapes and a saturated palette. Background is neutral dark gray so the food pops. The vibe is casual, playful, and approachable, the same family as Kenney's other "kit" packs (Mini Monsters, Tower Defense, etc.).

## Best for

- Cooking, restaurant, café, and diner games
- Farming, grocery, and market simulators
- Tycoon / management prototypes with food as a resource
- Educational apps about nutrition, food groups, or meal prep
- Top-down or isometric game prototypes needing a ready-made food library
- VR/AR kitchen demos (GLB ready for web viewers)
- UI mockups that need product/ingredient icons rendered in 3D
