# Smoke Particles

Cartoon-style 2D VFX sprite pack of pre-rendered smoke, explosion, flash, and puff effects. Frames are organized as flipbook sequences ready to drop into a particle system.

## Stats

- Assets: 70 (per package description)
- Zip contents: 86 files total
  - 79 PNG sprites (palette mode, transparent)
  - 1 `license.txt`
  - 1 `preview.png`, 1 `sample.png` (918×515 marketing images)
- Sprite subfolders (under `PNG/`):
  - `Black smoke/` — 25 frames
  - `Explosion/` — 9 frames
  - `Fart/` — 9 frames
  - `Flash/` — 9 frames
  - `White puff/` — 25 frames
- Individual sprite size: ~350–400px square (varies by frame)

## Visual style

Soft, hand-painted cartoon look with semi-transparent edges — the same illustrative family as other Kenney 2D packs. The cover sample shows a warm yellow-white fireball wrapped in a large dark smoke cloud against a flat blue sky background. Sprites are saved in palette mode with alpha, so they composite cleanly over any scene.

Tags: `cartoon`, `hand-painted`, `soft-edges`, `2D`, `sprite`, `vfx`, `flipbook`, `semi-transparent`, `illustrated`.

Palette anchors:

- Black smoke: dark grays to near-black (`#0d0d0d`–`#3a3a3a`)
- White puff: white to light gray (`#ffffff`–`#d0d0d0`)
- Warm flash: pale yellow core into orange (`#fff6c2`–`#ff6a00`)
- Sample background: flat blue (`#3a8bd6`)

## Best for

- 2D game VFX (explosions, smoke trails, muzzle flashes, hit impacts)
- Comedic fart/smoke poofs
- Fire, magic, and burst effects
- Flipbook-style particle systems in engines like Unity, Godot, or Phaser
- Any 2D project that needs ready-made atmospheric effects
