# Skyboxes

Five high-resolution equirectangular (cube-map) sky textures ready to drop into any 3D engine as a skybox — covering day, morning, night, alien, and space atmospheres in a clean, photoreal style.

## Stats
- Asset count: **5** skyboxes (day, morning, night, alien, space)
- Total files: **13** (8 PNGs, 1 TXT license, 3 URL link files, 1 Preview)
- Format breakdown: `.png` × 8, `.txt` × 1, `.url` × 3
- Top-level structure:
  - `License.txt`
  - `Preview.png` (918×515 contact sheet)
  - `Sample (Day).png`, `Sample (Night).png` (918×515 reference renders)
  - `Skyboxes/` — the 5 production textures, each **4096×2048** equirectangular PNG
  - `Instructions.url`, `Visit Kenney.url`, `Visit Patreon.url`

## Visual style
Photorealistic, modern, soft-handed — these read as illustrated photographs more than pixel art. The day sky is a calm gradient of light sky blue (`#85b3f0`) into paler cloud-tinted blues (`#abbde7`, `#b5c9f2`) with bright white sun and wispy, painterly cumulus; the night, alien, morning, and space variants shift the palette into deep blues, alien purples, warm dawn oranges, and starry blacks respectively, but all share the same high-resolution painted-cloud aesthetic. Designed for equirectangular (2:1 aspect) projection onto a sky cube/sphere.

## Best for

- Flying, racing, or space-flight simulators
