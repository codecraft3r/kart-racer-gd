# Top-down Tanks Remastered

A 2D top-down tank battle sprite pack with color-varied vehicles, military props, terrain tiles, and explosion FX — rendered in Kenney's signature clean cartoon style.

- **Sample tile size:** 20×28 to 24×24 px, RGBA

## Visual style

Flat-shaded vector cartoon with thick outlines, vibrant saturated colors, and high readability. Tanks are chunky and friendly with clearly distinguishable turret directions. The preview blends two biomes — bright grass green and warm sand tan — joined by a cool gray road. Burst effects use bright yellow star shapes. Palette: grass green, sand tan, road gray, primary red/blue, deep charcoal, warm brown, safety yellow.
