# Yellow Paint Pack

A small set of 10 bright yellow, flat vector-style paint graphics — dripping arrows, splat blots, splash bursts and dripping paint edges — designed to overlay onto platforms, walls, signage or 3D objects. Ships as PNG (transparent) and SVG so it scales cleanly from pixel-perfect 2D games to large 3D decals.

## Stats

- **Assets:** 10
- **Files in zip:** 47 (27 PNG, 13 SVG, 1 license, 2 URL links, 4 metadata)
- **PNG mode:** paletted (P) with transparency
- **Sprite sizes:** irregular — tightly cropped to painted outline (roughly 100–200 px on the longest side)
- **Vector source:** clean SVG in `Vector/Default/`
- **License:** CC0 1.0 (public domain)

## Visual style

- Flat, 2D vector look — no shading, no gradient inside the paint
- Bright single-tone yellow (~`#F5C518`) on transparent background
- Irregular painted silhouette with downward drips, evoking fresh, glossy wet paint
- High contrast against both light and dark backdrops
- Cartoon / casual game aesthetic; reads instantly as "caution / hazard / highlight"

## Best for

- Directional signage — arrows for wayfinding, level guidance or UI hints
- Hazard, warning or caution indicators (caution tape feel)
- Decorative splatter, splash and drip VFX on platforms, blocks and walls
- Painted-on labels, graffiti tags or "fresh paint" overlays
- In-world highlight markers, badges or collectible accents
