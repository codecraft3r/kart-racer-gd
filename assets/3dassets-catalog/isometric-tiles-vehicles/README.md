# Isometric Tiles Vehicles

A clean isometric (2:1 dimetric) tile set of a small town block: tan/sand modular ground tiles, dark asphalt roads with white lane markings, sidewalks, multi-story buildings (cream, brick red, warm brown, blue) with windows, awnings and grey rooftops, plus a fleet of low-poly vehicles — civilian cars in red/blue/green/yellow/silver plus service vehicles (ambulance, police, taxi, garbage truck). Soft flat shading, saturated accents on a deep blue backdrop. Each vehicle ships in 4 isometric directions and the pack includes combined spritesheets per color group and per vehicle class.

## Visual style

Isometric / dimetric (2:1), flat-shaded low-poly 3D, clean vector-style outlines, soft shadowing on the vehicle undersides, modular tileable base. Bright, slightly desaturated pastel palette with bold accent colors on the cars. Sits cleanly on a solid blue background — designed to compose into a full city scene.
