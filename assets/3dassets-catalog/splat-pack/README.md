# Splat Pack

A set of 30 cartoon paint-splat sprites in solid white on transparent backgrounds — drop-in particle overlays for 2D games, juice, and impact effects. Supplied as PNGs at 256px and 512px, plus matching SVGs.

## Stats

- **Asset count:** 30 (metadata) / 36 unique splat sprites in archive (`splat00`..`splat35`)
- **Total files in zip:** 117
  - PNG: 73 (36 × 256px + 36 × 512px + 1 Preview)
  - SVG: 37 (overview + 36 splat vectors)
  - Text/misc: 7 (license, URLs, directory entries)
- **Top-level folders:** `PNG/`, `Vector/`
- **Sprite sizes:** 256×256 and 512×512, palette-indexed with alpha
- **Preview image:** 918×515 RGBA

## Visual style

Flat, cartoon, monochrome white silhouettes on transparent backgrounds. Shapes are organic and hand-drawn — compact rounded blobs, spiky multi-prong eruptions, and scattered droplet patterns. No outlines, no shading; just clean vector-style blobs that tint and re-color freely at runtime. The marketing preview places them on a sky-blue (~#6FB7E1) backdrop.

## Best for

- 2D particle bursts and impact overlays
- Paint, liquid, slime or "blood" splat decals
- Cartoon juice and hit-feedback effects
- Water-droplet debris and explosion residue
- Decorative flourishes in UI or transitions
