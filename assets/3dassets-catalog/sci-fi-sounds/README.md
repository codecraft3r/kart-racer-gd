# Sci-fi Sounds

A curated pack of 73 free sci-fi sound effects (`.ogg`) by Kenney, including computer/terminal noise, door and hatch open/close, circular engines, lasers, robotic beeps, and more. Packaged as a single flat `Audio/` directory, perfect for retro-arcade and indie space-game projects.

## Stats

- **Files in zip:** 78 (1 license text, 2 `.url` links, 1 `desktop.ini`, 73 `.ogg` audio)
- **Audio assets:** 73 (format: OGG Vorbis)
- **Layout:** flat — all sounds sit in a single `Audio/` folder, no subfolders
- **Naming:** grouped prefixes with zero-padded index, e.g. `computerNoise_000..002`, `doorOpen_000..002`, `engineCircular_000..002`, `laser_000..`
- **License:** CC0 (public domain)

## Visual style

The preview is a flat, minimalist vector illustration on a vibrant **mint-green** background (`#3DDC97`). A pale circle holds a stylized **white-and-teal loudspeaker**, and a dark rounded-square badge in front shows a cartoon **UFO**. Rounded shapes, no gradients, bold silhouette — clearly communicates "audio + sci-fi" at a glance and matches the pack's retro-futuristic tone.

## Best for

- Sci-fi shooters, space sims, and roguelikes
- Retro arcade / 8-bit-styled games needing modern SFX
- Computer, terminal, and robot UIs (keyboard, glitch, scan)
- Drone, engine, and ambient loop beds
- Doors, hatches, locks, and mechanical interface feedback
- Lasers, zaps, power-ups, and weapon cues
