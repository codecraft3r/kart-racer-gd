# Mini Arcade

A 20-piece isometric 3D mini arcade set: low-poly arcade cabinets, characters, walls, floor and props, perfect for building cozy arcade-room scenes and mini-games.

## Stats

- Assets: 20
- Formats: FBX, GLB, OBJ (each model in all three)
- Texture: 512x512 colormap (two variations)
- Preview icons: 64x64 PNG per model
- Total files in zip: 123 (29 PNG, 20 FBX, 20 GLB, 20 OBJ, 20 MTL)

## Visual style

Low-poly, flat-shaded, isometric 3D with a vibrant, kid-friendly palette. No realism, no PBR — just clean, stylized geometry on a soft grey backdrop. Shared single colormap keeps every piece visually consistent.

Palette: orange (#FF7E44), cornflower blue (#6794D9), lavender purple (#A878E8), mint green (#61CB8B), magenta pink (#F378F0), gold yellow (#FFC044), tomato red (#CF534F), cream (#FDE4C7), light blue (#D0E8FF), cool grey (#868BA1).

## Best for

- Arcade room / scene builders (isometric or top-down)
- Mobile and casual mini-games
- Rapid prototyping of retail/entertainment spaces
- Kids, family or cozy-aesthetic game art
- Stylized 3D level mockups and design pitches
