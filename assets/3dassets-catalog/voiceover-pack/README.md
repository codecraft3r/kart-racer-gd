# Voiceover Pack

A free collection of 90 short English voiceover clips (Ogg Vorbis) for games and
interactive apps. Each line is recorded twice — once by a male voice talent and
once by a female voice talent — and covers the usual arcade/casual-game prompts:
round/match cues ("ready", "set", "go", "round 1"), outcomes ("you win",
"you lose", "game over", "mission completed/failed"), score/level feedback
("level up", "new highscore", "power up"), and a bundle of military-styled
callouts ("go go go", "cover me", "fire in the hole", "medic", "sniper", etc.).
Three preview files at the root let you audition the male and female voices
without unpacking the whole pack.

## Stats

- Asset count: 90 voice clips (advertised); zip holds 95 `.ogg` files total,
  including 3 preview clips at the root
- Format: Ogg Vorbis (`.ogg`)
- Voices: male + female versions of each line
- Zip layout: `Male/` and `Female/` folders, plus `Preview.ogg`,
  `Preview (Male).ogg`, `Preview (Female).ogg`, `License.txt`, `Credits.txt`
- Total files in archive: 101 (95 ogg, 2 txt, 2 url shortcuts, 2 directories)

## Visual style

- Flat vector illustration on a bright mint-green background (#3DD9A0-ish)
- Iconic white circular badge holding a stylized loudspeaker
- Dark charcoal speech/chat bubble accent with two white message lines
- Minimal, rounded, friendly — Kenney's signature clean-icon look
- No photography, no gradients beyond soft ambient shading
- Color palette: mint green, white, charcoal (#2D2D33), light grey

## Best for

- Casual / party / arcade games needing quick spoken feedback
- Tutorial and onboarding overlays
- Multiplayer match flow (rounds, ready/set/go, victory/defeat)
- Children's games and educational apps (clear, neutral delivery)
- Score / objective / power-up notifications
- Prototypes that need a friendly English voice layer without hiring talent
- Serious games, training sims, kiosks, museum interactives
