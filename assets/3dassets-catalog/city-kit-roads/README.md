# City Kit (Roads)

A modular set of isometric 3D road pieces — straight segments, curves, intersections, roundabouts, and elevated highway ramps with signage and props — designed to snap together into a stylized city network.

## Stats

- Assets: 70
- ZIP entries: 381
- Formats: FBX, GLB, OBJ (+ MTL), PNG textures
- Texture resolution: 512×512 (paletted)
- License: CC0 1.0

## Visual style

Clean, low-poly isometric 3D with smooth bevels and flat shading. Asphalt sits in cool, slightly desaturated grays with a soft lavender ambient tone; lane markings are crisp white; accent color comes from green overhead signs and a few warm orange traffic cones. The pieces read clearly at small sizes and tile predictably, which is the whole point of a modular road kit.

## Best for

- City builders and urban-planning prototypes
- Isometric or top-down casual / mobile games
- Stylized 3D racing or driving prototypes
- Scene mockups and level-blockout passes
- Educational or visualization projects that need a friendly road system
