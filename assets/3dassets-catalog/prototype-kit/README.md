# Prototype Kit

A pack of 145 low-poly 3D prototype assets for rapid level blockout and scene prototyping. Includes modular white primitives (cubes, cylinders, arches, ramps, stairs, walls, doors), orange vehicle placeholders (car, truck, jeep, motorbike), construction barriers, hazard markings, numerals 0-9, red door panels, and small blue humanoid character placeholders.

## Stats

- Assets: 145
- Total files in zip: 749
- Formats: FBX, GLB, OBJ (+ MTL + PNG textures)
- Texture sample: 512x512 colormaps and variation textures

## Visual style

Clean, low-poly, flat-shaded geometry on a dark gray checkered backdrop. Minimalist placeholder aesthetic with a focused palette of white/light gray primitives, orange props and vehicles, yellow hazard markings, red door panels, and blue character stand-ins. Modular pieces are designed to snap together for quick scene composition.

## Best for

- Level prototyping and greyboxing
- Game jam placeholders
- 3D scene mockups and concept visualization
- Teaching 3D asset pipelines
- Rapid iteration on game design
