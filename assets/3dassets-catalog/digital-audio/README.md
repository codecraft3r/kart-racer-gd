# Digital Audio

A compact pack of 60 short, retro/arcade-style sound effects by Kenney, delivered as Ogg Vorbis files. The set covers lasers, power-ups, phase jumps, zap/electric hits, pep blips and tonal UI cues — designed as drop-in feedback sounds for games, prototypes and apps.

## Stats

- **Asset count:** 60 (per package description)
- **Files in archive:** 68 (62 audio OGGs + Preview.ogg + license + 2 donation URLs + desktop.ini)
- **Audio format:** Ogg Vorbis
- **Sub-styles by name:** laser (9), phase (15), power (12), zap (6), pep (5), low (3), high (2), tone (1), misc (9)
- **License:** CC0 (Creative Commons Zero) — public domain, commercial use OK, no attribution required

## Visual style

The pack's branding is a flat, vector illustration in Kenney's signature minimal game-art style:

- Vibrant teal-green background (`#3DBB8B`)
- Central white circular badge with a stylized speaker/loudspeaker icon in white and pale blue-grey, bold dark outline
- A small dark charcoal rounded-square tag in the corner with a cartoon "audio bug" glyph
- Clean geometry, no gradients, no textures, high contrast

## Best for

- Indie and retro/arcade game SFX (lasers, power-ups, UI)
- Sci-fi shoot-em-ups, bullet hells, space shooters
- Power-up, level-up and pickup cues
- Menu button beeps, toggles, blips and confirmation sounds
- Prototyping placeholder audio before custom SFX are ready
