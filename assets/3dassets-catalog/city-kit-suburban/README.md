# City Kit (Suburban) · Kenney

A bright, low-poly isometric 3D kit of a suburban neighborhood: small white houses with vivid green roofs, asphalt roads, crosswalks, intersections, an overpass bridge, traffic cones, streetlights, conifer trees, orange fences, and rooftop solar panels. 40 ready-to-use assets, CC0 licensed.

## Visual style

- Isometric, low-poly 3D with a clean, cartoon, miniature-diorama look
- Diagrammatic geometry: flat shading, simple silhouettes, no fine texture noise
- Constrained palette: vivid green (roofs), off-white (walls), dark gray (some roofs), asphalt gray (roads), soft orange (fences, accents), teal-blue (windows), black (frames, road markings)
- Three style variations provided (Preview A/B/C) sharing the same shapes
- Friendly, approachable, board-game-meets-3D aesthetic — readable at small sizes
