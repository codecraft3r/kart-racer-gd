# Road Textures

A modular, top-down road and sidewalk tile set for city builders, driving sims, and isometric urban scenes. Tiles snap cleanly on a 64x64 grid and cover every connection you need to draw streets, intersections, roundabouts, dead-ends, and pedestrian pavements.

## Stats

- Assets: 90
- Files in archive: 288 (276 PNG, 2 SVG, 2 .url, 1 License.txt)
- Top-level folders: PNG (195), Legacy (84), Tilesheet (3), Vector (2)
- Tile size: 64x64 px (Legacy/PNG), also shipped as a 768x512 tilesheet plus a 1536x1024 @2x tilesheet
- Format: PNG (palette) + SVG vector master
- License: CC0 1.0

## Visual style

Flat, clean, top-down vector art. Dark asphalt roads (#2A2F33) sit on green grass (#3FA34D) for the drivable lane layer, while a second layer of light-blue pedestrian sidewalks (#7FC8E8) and sandy dirt patches (#F2D6A0) handles pavements, crossings, and plazas. White road markings, beige crosswalks, and small green nubs at every junction make connections obvious. Crisp edges, no shading or perspective, designed to tile seamlessly on a grid.
