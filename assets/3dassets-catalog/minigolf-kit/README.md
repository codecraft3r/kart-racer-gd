# Minigolf Kit

A stylized 3D minigolf course kit in Kenney's signature low-poly cartoon style. Includes modular fairway pieces, borders, ramps, bridges, a windmill, flags, balls, and obstacles — enough to assemble a full course in minutes. Ships in FBX, GLB, and OBJ formats with 512x512 palette-textured color maps.

## Visual style

Low-poly, flat-shaded, cartoon-stylized 3D models with vibrant, saturated colors. Bright mint-green fairways contrast with warm orange-coral border rails, complemented by wooden brown structural pieces, cream-tan bridge and obstacle blocks, and accent red/blue pin flags. Pieces are designed for isometric camera angles and read clearly against dark, neutral backgrounds.
