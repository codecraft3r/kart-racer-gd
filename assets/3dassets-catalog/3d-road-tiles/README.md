# 3D Road Tiles · Kenney

A modular low-poly 3D road and terrain tile set with asphalt streets, stone paths, rolling green hills, geometric trees, and water — designed for snapping together city, racetrack, and strategy-game levels.

## Stats

- **Assets:** 300 models
- **Formats:** glTF, OBJ + MTL, Unity package, Blender Python converter
- **Top-level entries:** Convert, Models, Unity package, License.txt, Preview.png, Sample.png
- **Zip contents:** 920 files total (302 glTF, 302 OBJ, 302 MTL, 2 PNG, 1 unitypackage, 1 .py, 2 .txt, 3 .url, 5 directory entries)

## Visual style

Low-poly, faceted geometry with flat-shaded surfaces and a bright, modern cartoon look. The palette pairs warm grass and stone tones with cool gray roads and water:

- Grass: `#8ab549`
- Asphalt: `#696969`
- Stone / sand: `#d8d3bf`
- Water: `#b0d7f6`
- Dirt: `#b89065`
- Highlights: `#ffffff`

## Best for

- City builders and town simulators
- Racing tracks and driving prototypes
- Top-down or isometric strategy games
- Level prototyping and greyboxing
