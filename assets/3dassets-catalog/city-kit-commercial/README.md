# City Kit (Commercial)

A low-poly isometric 3D city kit of modern commercial buildings — offices, shops, hotels, a glass tower, plazas, and a highway overpass — designed for fast city-builder and management-scam game scenes. Clean white/charcoal palette with blue glass accents and small green trees, in Kenney's signature flat-shaded stylized look.

## Stats

- 50 assets (per package description)
- 228 files inside the zip
- File types: 50 PNG (textures, 512×512 RGBA), 41 OBJ, 41 FBX, 41 GLB, 41 MTL, plus license / docs / URLs
- Ships in OBJ, FBX, and GLB formats with shared PNG colormap textures (two color variations)
- Source archive: `kenney_city-kit-commercial_2.1.zip`

## Visual style

- Low-poly, isometric 3D blocks with flat shading and soft window highlights
- Modern commercial urban feel: offices, shopfronts with awnings, hotels, tall tower, billboard, overpass
- Tight palette: white/cream walls, charcoal/gray roofs, light blue glass, gray roads, green trees and parks, occasional warm orange/red signage accents
- Clean, vector-look edges; readable at a distance; great for stylized city builders and casual 3D scenes

## Best for

- City-builder, tycoon, and management-scam games
- Isometric strategy and simulation games
- Mobile casual / hyper-casual urban scenes
- Low-poly 3D animations, prototypes, and VR/Mockup shots
- Drop-in commercial block compositions: downtown blocks, shopping streets, business districts
