# Modular Buildings

Stylized low-poly 3D building kit featuring 3-4 story apartment blocks, gabled houses, and shopfronts designed to snap together for procedural city, town, and village scenes.

## Stats

- **Assets:** 100 modular pieces
- **Files in archive:** 563
- **Model formats:** GLB, OBJ (+MTL), FBX
- **Textures:** 117 PNGs (512×512 palette/colormap, plus variation-a / variation-b)
- **Previews:** Preview.png + Preview (Variation A) + Preview (Variation B) at 918×515

## Visual style

Low-poly, stylized, flat-shaded with cartoon proportions and an isometric-friendly camera. Soft pastel palette of cream/peach walls, dark slate-blue gabled roofs, pastel blue windows, mint-green awnings, and warm tan doors/trim. Two recolor variations included for instant palette swaps. Designed to tile cleanly for blocky city layouts.

## Best for

- City builders and tycoon games
- Tower defense and strategy games
- Mobile 3D projects and casual sims
- VR / desktop virtual worlds
- Rapid greybox and prototype blockouts
