# Space Station Kit

Modular low-poly 3D kit for building sci-fi space stations. Isometric, cartoon-shaded, and game-ready with OBJ, FBX and GLB exports plus matching colormap textures.

## Stats

- Asset count: 90
- Archive entries: 506 (.glb 97, .fbx 97, .obj 97, .mtl 97, .png 104, plus docs/links)
- Texture resolution: 512×512 colormap + 64×64 per-piece previews
- Formats included: OBJ, FBX, GLB
- License: CC0 (public domain)

## Visual style

Low-poly isometric 3D with clean cartoon shading and a lavender-and-orange palette against a dark grid backdrop. Pieces are chunky, rounded, and slot together as modular floors, walls, stairs, railings, pipes, consoles, seating, equipment, crates, ladders, antennas, and decorative asteroid rocks. Uniform scale and consistent lighting make pieces drop-in compatible for kit-bashing.

## Best for

- Sci-fi base builders and station simulators
- Space-themed level design and prototypes
- Isometric strategy, tycoon and management games
- Modular scene building for VR/3D educational demos
- Rapid greyboxing of interior sci-fi environments
