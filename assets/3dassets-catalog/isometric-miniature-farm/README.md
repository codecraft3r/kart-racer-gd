# Isometric Miniature Farm

A cozy, hand-painted isometric prop kit for building pastoral farm and countryside game scenes. Includes barns, fences, decking, hay bales, corn fields, dirt tiles, ladders, chimneys and more — 60 unique assets, all rendered from a fixed isometric angle.

## Stats

- **Assets:** 60
- **Files in archive:** 466 (459 PNGs, 2 inner ZIPs, plus readme/URL)
- **Top-level folders:** `Angle/`, `Isometric/`, `Samples/`
- **Format:** PNG (RGBA / indexed), 256×512 base tiles, multi-angle variants (N/E/S/W)
- **License:** CC0 1.0 (public domain, no attribution required)

## Visual style

Isometric 2:1 projection with a hand-painted, slightly stylized look. Detailed wood-grain and plank textures on buildings, soft and even lighting, and a warm, earthy palette of saddle and tan browns accented by the green of corn plants and muted stone grey. Props feel like a tabletop diorama — cozy, miniature, and pastoral rather than realistic or high-fantasy.

## Best for

- Farm, countryside, or rural-themed game scenes
- Isometric casual / tycoon / city-builder prototypes
- Cozy or pastoral mobile or web games
- Level art for barns, fences, fields, decking, and farm props
- Map mockups, dioramas, and isometric scene demos
