# City Kit (Industrial)

A modular set of 25 stylized low-poly 3D industrial buildings — warehouses, factories, refineries, smokestacks, storage tanks, pipes, and road infrastructure — rendered in a clean isometric view on a consistent grid. Cool gray and white surfaces accented by orange factory doors and tanks, with small green trees for life, give the kit a friendly, toy-like quality that still reads as a believable industrial district.

## Visual style

Low-poly, flat-shaded, isometric 3D with no outlines and soft, even lighting. Voxel-ish massing keeps silhouettes blocky and readable from a top-down or ¾ perspective. Palette is dominated by cool grays, whites, and slate roads, with warm orange accents on doors, tanks, and pipes, and small green trees for a touch of life. Models are designed to tile and snap together on a modular grid, making them well suited to a coherent city block.

## Best for

- City-builder and tycoon-style games
- Isometric strategy and management games
- Factory, warehouse, or logistics simulations
- Top-down prototypes and greybox scenes
- Modular dioramas and industrial-district environments
